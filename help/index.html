Help

Questions the guide doesn't answer: email supportholewise@gmail.com.

Distances look off

Check that Location and Precise Location are on (Settings > Permissions). Out in the trees GPS can wander for a few seconds; give it a moment.

Meters or yards

Settings > General > Distance units. It changes every number in the app, including your bag.

Playing with no signal

Download the course first from its course page (Download for offline). Your round is saved on the phone and syncs when you're back online.

Club advice feels wrong

Club advice is only as good as your bag. Set your carry distances in Profile > My clubs. HoleWise also learns from the shots you mark.

Bought Plus but it's not showing

Sign in to the same HoleWise account, then Settings > Restore purchases.

Cancel Plus

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings > Manage subscription, or your App Store account settings.

Delete your account

Settings > Delete account. Everything goes, permanently. Cancel Plus in the App Store as well.
