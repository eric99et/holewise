HoleWise

Privacy Policy

Who we are

HoleWise is a golf app for distances, scores, stats and games with friends. HoleWise is responsible for the personal data described here. Questions go to supportholewise@gmail.com.

What we collect

Account: your email address, name, profile photo if you add one, and how you sign in (Apple or email and password).

Golf: handicap index, home course, playing hand, clubs and carry distances, rounds, scores, shots you mark, games, crews, challenges and badges.

Friends: who you are friends with, requests, invites and the rounds you choose to share.

Purchases: whether you have Plus. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card.

Location, camera and photos

Location is used only while you use HoleWise, to measure distances and find nearby courses. The positions of shots you mark are saved with the round.

The camera is used to mark today's pin and for Range sessions. Images and video are processed on your phone and never uploaded.

Photos: only the single picture you choose as your profile photo leaves the phone. HoleWise has no access to the rest of your library.

Why we use it

To run the app you asked for: keep your rounds and stats, give club advice, sync between your devices, and show friends what you share with them. The legal basis is our agreement with you (the Terms of Use).

Usage analytics only if you turn on "Share anonymous usage analytics" in Settings. It is off by default and never includes your name, email or location. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

We do not sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps.

Who helps us

Supabase stores accounts and app data on servers in the EU (Stockholm).

Apple provides Sign in with Apple, payments and weather. Open-Meteo provides weather when Apple's is unavailable; it receives the course position, not who you are.

Course maps come from OpenStreetMap. Course searches send the search text or the map area, never your account.

Account emails (confirmation, password reset) are sent through an email delivery service on our behalf.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. Delete your account in Settings and your profile, rounds, clubs and photos are permanently deleted. As a guest, your data stays on the phone until you delete the app or withdraw consent in Settings.

Your rights

You can get a copy of your data (Settings > Export my data), correct it, delete it (Settings > Delete account), withdraw consent (Settings > Withdraw consent & delete data) and object to processing. Email us for anything else.

If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Children

HoleWise is not meant for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app asks you to read and accept the new version.
